01 — The project

Forestry designed as an investment asset.

EverRoot combines land, fast-growing forestry and downstream processing into a single long-term agricultural asset.

The initial concept is a 35-hectare Paulownia plantation in Southern Bulgaria, developed progressively rather than planted all at once. This creates a staged operating model and allows the project to build capacity over time.

01

EU-based asset

Land and operations planned within the European Union.

02

Regenerative cycle

Paulownia can regenerate from the established root system after harvest, supporting subsequent production cycles.

03

Diversified revenue

Intercropping, beekeeping and timber create different economic phases.

04

Planned production system

Drip irrigation, soil-led fertilisation and mycorrhiza at planting are included in the production plan.

05

Lean operating plan

The year-one plan combines a three-person team with outsourced agronomy and accounting support.

02 — Business model

Value is built in stages.

The plantation is designed to generate supporting agricultural income before the main timber harvest, while the long-term value comes from premium wood and processing.
